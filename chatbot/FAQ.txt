Q) What kind of meters does KE use?
A) KE uses two types of digital meters: single-phase (for usage under 5kW) and three-phase (for 5kW or more). All meters meet international standards and are tested in KE’s certified lab before and after installation.

Q) I feel my meter is running fast?
A) If you think your meter is running fast, you can:
1. Request a meter test at KE’s lab (a small fee applies).
2. Install your own market-bought meter to compare readings.


Q)What safety measures has KE taken for public and staff safety?
KE has taken several steps to improve safety for both citizens and staff:
1. Pole Grounding Upgrade: Replaced old copper grounding with galvanized steel to prevent theft.
2. ABC Installation: Converted 9,000 PMTs to insulated Aerial Bundled Cables (ABC) to reduce electric shocks and theft risks.
3. Illegal Connections Removal: Removed over 200,000 illegal Kundas and 10,000 illegal streetlight switches in 2020.
Q) What is Fuel Charge Adjustment (FCA)?
1. Fuel prices change every month based on global markets. Because of this, electricity companies are allowed to adjust their prices to match fuel costs. This is called the Fuel Charge Adjustment (FCA).
Here’s how it works:
1. FCA is set by NEPRA, the national power regulator, for all power companies including KE.
2. It reflects monthly changes in fuel prices and power generation costs.
3. The adjustment can be positive or negative, depending on whether fuel prices went up or down.
4. KE applies for FCA every month, and after a public hearing, NEPRA verifies and approves it.
5. FCA is then added to or subtracted from your monthly bill.


Q) What is the difference between single-phase and three-phase electricity?
A)
1. Single-phase electricity is commonly used in homes. It powers things like lights, fans, and small appliances.

2. Three-phase electricity is used in factories, offices, and big buildings. It’s stronger and powers heavy machines or large equipment.
Q) How can I check my electricity bill online?
 A) Go to the KLink website, log in your account, click on Billing Services, then select View Bill.
Q) Why is my electricity bill higher this month?
 A) A higher bill could be due to increased usage, seasonal changes, or fuel price adjustments. You can call 118 for a detailed bill breakdown.
Q) What is the due date for bill payment?
 A) The due date is mentioned on your bill. Pay before this date to avoid late fees.
Q) Can I pay my bill in installments?
 A) Yes, KE offers installment plans for eligible customers. Go to the main page → billing → choose mode to installment and select 10 or 15 day installment as per your need. 
Q) How can I update my contact details?

Q) How can I register a complaint online?
